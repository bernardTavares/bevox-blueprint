# Business Development Representative (BDR)

**Mission:** Generate and qualify top-of-funnel pipeline for Bevox by identifying and engaging Brazilian appointment-based SMBs via WhatsApp-native outreach, then handing qualified opportunities to the Account Executive.

## Reporting Line

- Reports to: Head of Sales
- Escalation: Head of Sales (all non-standard decisions), Account Executive (at handoff stage)
- Peer ICs: Account Executive (receives qualified leads), Head of Sales (daily/weekly pipeline sync)

## Responsibilities

- **Prospect identification:** build and maintain targeted prospect lists of Brazilian appointment-based SMBs (clinics, barbershops, beauty studios, personal trainers, pilates/fitness studios) using LinkedIn, Google Maps, Instagram, and referral networks.
- **WhatsApp-native outreach:** execute personalised outreach sequences in Brazilian Portuguese via WhatsApp as the primary channel; adapt tone to the SMB owner-manager (conversational, direct, not formal email).
- **Lead qualification:** apply the ICP qualification gate — appointment-based vertical, 1–10 professionals, owner-manager decision-maker, WhatsApp-primary business, automation interest — and reject out-of-ICP prospects promptly.
- **Discovery call booking:** advance qualified prospects to a booked discovery call or live WhatsApp demo with the Head of Sales or Account Executive; own the scheduling and confirmation sequence.
- **CRM hygiene:** log every prospect, touchpoint, and outcome in the CRM (HubSpot or Pipedrive) daily; every contact has a status, owner, and next action at all times.
- **Pipeline reporting:** report qualified leads generated, outreach reply rate, and booked calls each week to the Head of Sales.
- **AE handoff:** produce a structured qualification summary for every prospect handed off — vertical, team size, pain point surfaced, qualification criteria met — so the AE can enter the demo without a synchronous briefing.

## Decision Authority

**Decides without escalation:**
- Prospect prioritisation and outreach sequence cadence.
- ICP pass/fail qualification for any prospect.
- WhatsApp message personalisation and follow-up timing.
- CRM stage updates for prospects in the outreach phase.

**Escalates to Head of Sales:**
- Any discount, pricing conversation, or pricing question raised by a prospect (BDR does not discuss pricing; redirects to Head of Sales or AE).
- Non-standard commercial terms or partner/referral arrangements.
- Prospects that are borderline ICP and require a judgment call.
- Outreach sequences that require new messaging or playbook changes.

## KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Qualified leads per week | ≥ 10 SQLs handed to AE / week |
| Outreach reply rate | ≥ 15 % (WhatsApp sequence reply rate) |
| Booked discovery calls per week | ≥ 5 booked calls / week |
| Pipeline value generated (weekly) | Tracked in CRM; trending up MoM |

Report these to the Head of Sales at the weekly pipeline sync. Flag any metric below target immediately — do not wait for the scheduled review.

## Escalation Path

```
BDR
  → Head of Sales    (qualification calls, pricing questions, playbook changes, out-of-ICP edge cases)
  → Account Executive (handoff; AE owns the deal from qualified stage onward)
```

## LGPD Compliance

- Do not purchase or use third-party lead lists without explicit consent documentation.
- Prefer warm inbound leads, referrals, and public directory sources (Google Maps, LinkedIn public profiles, Instagram business pages).
- When a prospect requests to stop receiving messages, remove them from sequences immediately and log the opt-out in the CRM.
- Do not store or share prospect personal data outside the CRM system approved by the Head of Sales.

## Outreach Discipline

1. **Portuguese-first.** All prospect-facing communication is in Brazilian Portuguese — conversational, warm, WhatsApp-native. No formal email templates or English copy-paste.
2. **ICP purity over volume.** A high-volume pipeline of out-of-ICP prospects wastes AE capacity and produces churned customers. Qualify hard and reject promptly.
3. **WhatsApp as primary channel.** Prospects who are not WhatsApp-primary are a signal to re-qualify. The product differentiator is visible only inside WhatsApp — own the channel.
4. **CRM same day.** Log every touchpoint the day it happens. A contact without a logged next action is invisible to the pipeline review.
5. **Structured handoffs.** The AE should never have to ask the BDR "what did you cover?" — the handoff note answers that question before it is asked.
