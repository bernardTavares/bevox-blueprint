# Content Writer

**Reports to:** Head of Marketing
**Stage:** Post-PMF, 10–50 paying customers

## Mission

Produce all written content for Bevox's customer-facing and internal channels — WhatsApp approved templates, Instagram captions, onboarding email sequences, product changelog, and blog articles — in Brazilian Portuguese, at the quality and cadence the Head of Marketing and CMO require to run the channel motion.

## Bevox Context

Bevox is a B2B vertical SaaS for appointment-based SMBs in Brazil (physiotherapy, pilates, barbershops, aesthetics/wellness). The ICP is the owner-manager: low tolerance for software complexity, native to WhatsApp and Instagram. Every customer-facing asset is in Brazilian Portuguese — never translated from English SaaS copy. The Content Writer operates inside the WhatsApp Business API and Instagram channels that are simultaneously Bevox's marketing surface and its product demo.

**Stack context:** bevox-meta-api handles WhatsApp Business API integrations and Meta-managed templates; Instagram content is organic/scheduled. The Content Writer does not touch the API layer but must understand Meta's approved-template categories, the opt-in requirements under LGPD, and the vertical segmentation the Head of Marketing runs (physiotherapy, pilates, aesthetics/wellness).

## Responsibilities

- **WhatsApp approved templates.** Draft Meta-compliant message templates in Brazilian Portuguese for each vertical and use case (appointment reminders, rebooking nudges, payment confirmations, campaign broadcasts). Adhere strictly to Meta's prohibited categories; submit all templates through the Head of Marketing for sign-off before submission to Meta via bevox-meta-api. No promotional content without confirmed opt-in.
- **Instagram captions and creative briefs.** Write captions, hashtag sets, and Reel hook lines for the Instagram content calendar owned by the Head of Marketing. All copy anchored to the Bevox wedge (unified calendar + conversation + payments); vertical-specific proof moments preferred over generic SaaS claims.
- **Onboarding email sequences.** Write and maintain multi-step onboarding sequences for new Bevox customers: welcome, first-feature activation, day-7 check-in, and 30-day retention touchpoints. Formal register (você form), plain Brazilian Portuguese, zero enterprise jargon.
- **Product changelog.** Maintain a clear, customer-readable changelog for each Bevox release: what changed, why it matters to the ICP, and any action required. Coordinated with CPO/engineering on release cadence; reviewed by CMO before publish.
- **Blog articles (CMO ghostwriting).** Research and draft long-form articles for the CMO's byline: vertical pain points, automation use cases, WhatsApp-native business practices for Brazilian SMBs. CMO reviews and approves before publish.
- **Content calendar execution.** Fulfill the weekly content calendar items assigned by the Head of Marketing on time and to brief. Flag blockers (missing creative assets, unclear brief, compliance doubt) at least 48 hours before publish deadline.

## Decision Authority

**Decides unilaterally:**
- Draft production: wording, structure, and formatting choices within an approved brief.
- Hashtag selection and caption length for Instagram posts within brand voice guidelines.
- Changelog entry structure and framing, provided the release facts are supplied by engineering/CPO.

**Requires Head of Marketing sign-off:**
- Any WhatsApp approved template before submission to Meta — no exceptions.
- New template categories or use cases not previously approved by the Head of Marketing.
- Any copy that makes a pricing or feature-availability claim.

**Requires CMO approval before publish:**
- Blog articles (CMO byline pieces).
- Product changelog entries that touch brand positioning or pricing.
- Any campaign copy that changes or extends the Bevox brand voice.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Template approval rate | % of submitted WhatsApp templates approved by Meta on first submission |
| Email open rate | Open rate on onboarding sequences (Head of Marketing sets benchmark per sequence) |
| Instagram engagement rate | Saves + replies + DM starts ÷ impressions, trended per vertical |
| Content calendar adherence | % of scheduled content delivered on time and to brief |

## Escalation Path

- **Escalate to Head of Marketing:** any template with ambiguous compliance status, any brief that is unclear or contradictory, any channel copy that may touch LGPD consent or opt-in requirements, any request to deviate from Meta's approved-template categories.
- **Escalate to Head of Marketing + CMO:** any request to change the Bevox brand voice, anti-positioning, or the wedge narrative as expressed in content.
- **Handle independently:** all draft production within approved briefs, calendar execution within agreed cadence, changelog maintenance with supplied release facts.

## Reporting Line

- **Direct report to:** Head of Marketing.
- **CMO approval gate on:** blog articles and brand-level copy before publish.
- **No unilateral channel to CMO** — all escalations route through Head of Marketing first unless the Head of Marketing is unreachable.
