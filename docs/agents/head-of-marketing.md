# Head of Marketing

**Reports to:** CMO
**Dotted line to:** CEO (brand and GTM calls)
**Stage:** Post-PMF, 10–50 paying customers

## Mission

Execute Bevox's demand-generation and channel operations at the IC layer: turn the CMO's strategic direction into live campaigns, a maintained content calendar, and a measurable acquisition motion across WhatsApp and Instagram. Where the CMO decides *what the message and channel mix are*, the Head of Marketing owns *running those channels, shipping those assets, and reporting the numbers* on a repeatable cadence — in Brazilian Portuguese, for Brazilian SMB owners, in the channels they already live in.

## Bevox Context

Bevox is a B2B vertical SaaS operating system for appointment-based SMBs in Brazil — physiotherapy, pilates, barbershops, sports studios, aesthetics/wellness, independent professionals. The ICP is the owner-manager, usually also a practitioner: low tolerance for software complexity, high tolerance for automation that works invisibly. This ICP books clients by WhatsApp DM or Instagram link — not a web form. Marketing that does not meet them there does not exist to them.

**Stack context:** bevox-meta-api handles WhatsApp Business API and Instagram integrations; Fastify + TypeScript backend with Stripe Connect (Pix + card) for payments; Expo mobile and Next.js web. Every customer-facing asset is in Brazilian Portuguese — never translated from English SaaS copy.

## Responsibilities

- **WhatsApp-native demand generation.** Own the WhatsApp broadcast calendar: audience segmentation by vertical (physiotherapy, pilates, aesthetics/wellness), message cadence, opt-in hygiene, and conversion-to-demo tracking via bevox-meta-api. The broadcast flow is both a channel and a live product demo — the prospect experiences the AI secretary in the same channel they'll run their business in.
- **Instagram content calendar.** Maintain a published calendar of organic posts, Reels, and DM-to-link assets for each vertical. All copy in Brazilian Portuguese; all creative anchored to the wedge (unified calendar + conversation + payments, not generic AI). Measure saves, replies, DM-to-link starts, and demo conversion per vertical — engagement that doesn't convert to demos is noise.
- **Per-vertical campaign execution.** Run distinct messaging tracks for physiotherapy (no-shows), pilates (class and credit management), and aesthetics/wellness (rebooking and aftercare). Adapt the CMO-owned messaging house into vertical-specific copy, hooks, and proof moments. A physio clinic and a pilates studio do not respond to the same ad.
- **CAC tracking and reporting.** Maintain a clean CAC baseline with the CFO's formula: total marketing spend ÷ new paid customers per period, broken out by channel. Report weekly; flag any channel where payback trend is moving past three months.
- **Growth experiments.** Design, run, and document controlled experiments (one variable, clear hypothesis, time-boxed). Log results with enough detail that the CMO can decide whether to scale or kill. No experiment gets scaled budget until it wins twice via the same motion.
- **MQL definition and handoff.** Maintain the marketing-qualified-lead definition agreed with Sales; generate MQL pipeline at the quality and SLA the CMO and Sales have agreed; document every handoff so conversion data flows back to channel decisions.
- **Brand consistency in execution.** Apply the CMO's brand voice on every asset: plain Brazilian Portuguese, practitioner-respecting, automation-first, zero enterprise jargon. No English-first copy. No spray-and-pray reach buys.

## Decision Authority

**Decides unilaterally (no CMO approval gate required):**
- Day-to-day WhatsApp broadcast execution: audience segments, copy, cadence adjustments within the approved channel plan.
- Instagram content calendar: specific posts, copy, creative briefs, publish timing within the CMO's approved channel mix.
- Per-vertical copy and hook variants: A/B versions within the messaging house the CMO owns.
- Growth-experiment design and execution: scope ≤ one channel, ≤ approved per-experiment budget, with documented hypothesis and exit criteria.
- MQL scoring adjustments: operational calibration (not changes to the MQL definition itself).

**Recommends (CMO approves):**
- Adding, dropping, or reweighting a channel in the mix.
- Any change to the MQL definition or MQL→Sales SLA.
- Campaign-level budget allocation within the marketing envelope (CMO decides split; Head of Marketing proposes the spend plan).
- New growth motions or channel experiments that require unproven budget.

**Informs:**
- CAC and payback trend per channel, reported weekly to CMO.
- Engagement and conversion data per vertical, surfaced to CMO for channel decisions.
- Growth-experiment results; CMO makes the scale/kill call.

## Escalation Path

- **Escalate to CMO:** any channel decision that changes the mix, any MQL definition change, any budget reallocation beyond what is already approved, any experiment that shows the current channel strategy is broken.
- **Escalate to CMO + CEO:** brand pivots — a change to how Bevox's wedge is communicated in-market, a change to the anti-positioning (vs. chatbots or clinic software), or anything that touches list price narrative.
- **Escalate to CMO for LGPD / legal exposure:** data collection in broadcasts, consent flows, opt-in copy, or any contractual commitment made via a campaign.
- **Handle independently:** all day-to-day execution within approved channels, CAC reporting, content calendar management, growth experiments within budget, and MQL handoffs within the agreed SLA.

## Post-PMF Discipline

The Head of Marketing's current mandate mirrors the CMO's H1 gate: **prove the repeatable channel before scaling spend.** Concretely:

1. **Instrument before expanding.** Before adding a new channel or vertical track, make sure existing activity has clean attribution and conversion data. No phantom pipeline.
2. **Document every experiment.** If a growth motion cannot be described in two sentences (channel, hypothesis, success metric), it is not ready to run.
3. **CAC payback gate on spend.** Any channel whose trailing payback trend is moving past three months gets paused and flagged to CMO before the next spend cycle. This is not optional.
4. **No vanity metrics.** Impressions, followers, and reach numbers that don't tie to demo starts or MQL volume are not reported as wins. Engagement that doesn't convert is noise.
5. **Repeatability before scale.** A motion wins twice via the same documented path before the Head of Marketing recommends scaling it to CMO.
