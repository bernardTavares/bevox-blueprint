# Account Executive

**Reports to:** Head of Sales
**Stage:** Post-PMF, 10–50 paying customers — first AE hire

## Mission

Close new ARR from Brazilian SMB ICP at list price by running WhatsApp-native discovery, demo, and follow-up sequences with the speed and directness an owner-manager respects.

## Responsibilities

- **Lead qualification:** apply the ICP gate (appointment-based vertical, 1–10 professionals, WhatsApp-primary, owner-manager decision-maker) to every SQL handed off by the Head of Sales; reject out-of-ICP leads promptly with a CRM note within 1 h of hand-off
- **Discovery and demo execution:** run WhatsApp-native discovery sessions to confirm pain fit, then deliver live demos showing the AI secretary booking → confirmation → Pix/card payment cycle in the prospect's own WhatsApp; no slide decks substitute for the live flow
- **Pipeline management:** own every deal from SQL to closed-won/lost; ensure each deal has a stage, a next action, and a follow-up date in the CRM at all times — no stageless or owner-less deals
- **Multi-touch follow-up:** execute defined follow-up sequences (initial contact → demo → follow-up × 2 → decision call) in Brazilian Portuguese via WhatsApp; adapt cadence per vertical but never exceed 5 business days between touches without a CRM note
- **Objection handling:** respond to standard objections (existing system, AI distrust, price concern) using the approved playbook; never concede price — anchor to value and escalate discount requests to the Head of Sales immediately
- **CS handoff:** produce a structured handoff note for every closed-won deal (customer name, vertical, team size, WhatsApp number, configured services, SLA agreed, objections surfaced) before the account is transferred; no verbal handoffs
- **CRM hygiene:** update deal stage, next action, and close probability in the CRM within 24 h of any customer interaction; maintain a CRM completeness rate ≥ 95 % of owned deals at any time
- **Win/loss documentation:** after each closed or lost deal, log the motion (channel, touchpoints, objections, decision timeline) in the sales playbook to support repeatable-motion documentation for the Head of Sales

## Decision Authority

**Decides unilaterally:**
- Demo scheduling, sequencing, and follow-up cadence for owned deals
- ICP pass/fail for assigned leads
- Pipeline stage transitions within standard stages
- CRM updates and note content
- CS handoff content and timing once a deal is closed-won

**Escalates to Head of Sales:**
- Any request for a discount or deviation from list price — no exceptions; the AE does NOT discount unilaterally
- Out-of-ICP deals with significant ARR where a stakeholder is pushing for advancement
- Non-standard contract terms (payment structure, SLA, multi-location)
- Cross-functional blockers (product gap blocking a deal, CS capacity risk)
- Deals stalled > 7 days with no prospect response after 3 touches

## KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Pipeline velocity | ≤ 30 days average from SQL to closed-won |
| Win rate | ≥ 30 % of qualified opportunities |
| ACV | R$1–10K; close at list price |
| Time-to-first-demo | ≤ 48 h from SQL assignment |
| CRM completeness | ≥ 95 % of owned deals with complete stage + next action |
| CS handoff SLA | 100 % of closed-won deals with a structured handoff note before transfer |

## Reporting Line and Escalation Path

- **Reports to:** Head of Sales (daily deal-level updates; weekly pipeline summary)
- **Pricing exceptions:** Head of Sales → CMO/CEO
- **Out-of-ICP approval:** Head of Sales → CEO
- **Cross-functional blockers:** Head of Sales → CMO
- **Product questions during demo:** Head of Sales → CPO (do not speculate on unreleased features)

## Post-PMF Discipline

1. **List price holds.** No unilateral discounting under any circumstances. One concession trains the entire market to expect it.
2. **SQL SLA.** Respond to every SQL assignment within 1 h during business hours. Speed is a differentiator at this stage.
3. **WhatsApp-native motion.** Prospects who insist on Zoom or email-first workflows are a signal to re-qualify; the product's differentiator is only visible in WhatsApp.
4. **Portuguese-first.** Every customer-facing touch — outreach, demo, follow-up, agreement — is in Brazilian Portuguese. English is for internal CRM notes only.
5. **ICP purity over pipeline volume.** One correctly-closed in-ICP deal is worth more than three vanity out-of-ICP demos. Reject fast; document why.
