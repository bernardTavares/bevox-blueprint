# Department: People

## C-suite Owner

**CHRO** — owns the agent org itself, role design, agent-catalog hygiene, and the first 1–3 human hires.

## Lead

**[Head of People](../docs/agents/head-of-people.md)** — agent-org maintenance, onboarding the first human hires, and hiring-process execution under the CHRO.

## IC Roles (Team)

No IC agents instantiated at this stage. The Head of People carries all operational execution directly. ICs (recruiter, people-ops specialist) earn their seat only when human headcount grows beyond the first 1–3 hires.

## OKRs Owned

From `okrs/q4-corporate.md`:

- **KR3.5** — Hire readiness: `processes/hiring.md` authored and current, time-to-hire ≤ 30 days, zero duplicate/ambiguous-owner agent roles in the catalog (Owner: CHRO)

## Processes Owned

From `raci-matrix.md`:

- **Hiring** (`processes/hiring.md`) — CHRO is **R** for running the hiring process end to end (CEO is **A** for the decision to open a role)
