**CFO** — LGPD compliance, contracts, and payment-regulation risk sit close to finance. Head of Legal reports primarily to the CFO with a dotted line to the COO (operational/process governance).

## Lead

**[Head of Legal](../docs/agents/head-of-legal.md)** — LGPD, terms of service, PSP/Connect contractual and regulatory exposure under the CFO.

No IC agents instantiated at this stage. The Head of Legal carries all operational execution directly. ICs earn their seat only when the legal/compliance workload outgrows a single lead (likely at 50+ customers with multi-jurisdiction expansion).
